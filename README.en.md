# Hopper

A pixel art jumping game: a grasshopper hops from leaf to leaf, ever higher, from the garden to the sky, dusk, night and on to the stars.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Costumes, rocket, bubble and spiders (new in 1.2.0)

- **Seven costumes** won by playing, to pick in the panel: Green, **Golden** (crown), **Pink mantis** (flower), **Night** (wizard hat), **Ladybird**, **Robot** (antenna) and **Rainbow**, which keeps changing colour. Each one tells you what is left to do to unlock it.
- The **rocket** (above 250 m) blasts the grasshopper very high and squashes everything in its way.
- The **bubble** protects her from one sting.
- **Spiders** (above 260 m) go up and down on their thread: jump on them, don't touch them from the side.

## Controls

- **← →** (or **Q D**): steer the grasshopper. It jumps by itself.
- **Mouse or finger**: press to the left or right of the grasshopper.
- **P** or **Esc**: pause.

## How it works

- **Green** leaves hold, **blue** ones slide sideways, **dry** ones snap underfoot and **clouds** only carry you once.
- A **red mushroom** sends it very high. **Dragonfly wings** let it fly for a while.
- Higher up come the **wasps**: jump on them to squash them, but don't touch them from the side.
- It goes from one side of the screen to the other. If it falls off the bottom, it's over.
- The higher you go, the further apart the leaves are, and the more they move.

## Stats

The Bests panel keeps your **best** in metres, **total metres**, **wasps squashed** and the number of **games**.

## Data and sync

Your bests are saved by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs if sync is on.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → Hopper → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).

## Scenery, ladybug and full screen

- **The scenery changes as you climb**: tall grass in the garden, then trees, big clouds, and the moon at the top. Leaves have their tip, veins and stem.
- **The friendly ladybug**: catch it and it carries you up for a while. Wasps can't sting you meanwhile.
- **Full screen**: the "FULL SCREEN" button. The game takes the full height of the window.
